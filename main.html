Dilmate AI Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Dilmate AI respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

Dilmate AI does not require user registration and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Local Storage

Your selected profile, settings, and chat history are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and Room Database.

This information remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

AI Conversations

All companion conversations are generated locally using pre-written conversation data included within the application. Messages are not sent to any external AI service or cloud server.

Video Content

The app contains pre-recorded AI companion videos for entertainment purposes. These are not live video calls with real people.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app performance and fix technical issues. These services may collect anonymous diagnostic and usage information.

Advertisements

Dilmate AI uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and advertising information according to Google's own privacy practices.

Security

Any online resources used by the application, such as media files, are accessed securely using HTTPS connections.

Children's Privacy

Dilmate AI is intended for users who meet the minimum age required under applicable laws and Google Play policies.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
